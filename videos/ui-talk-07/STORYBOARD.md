## Video direction

Nine hard cuts. The ground is cream paper (`#FFF9F0`) with a faint dot grid, and it never leaves. Every other object starts hidden, enters in reading order (rise or soft scale, `power3.out`), holds, then leaves upward in reverse order before the next cut. No voice, no music bed, no photos. Sound is a short whoosh or pop on the hero of the scene. Crayon wobble is on strokes only.

Palette and type: `frame.md`. Highlight only 「UI 용어집」, 「Stepper, Sidebar」, and 「이 표」.
